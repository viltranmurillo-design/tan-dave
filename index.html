The Movement

Built For The Hustle, Not The Hype

Heavyweight By Default

Premium 100% heavy cotton at 220–240 GSM. The fabric holds its shape through daily wear and repeated washing.

Reinforced Everywhere

Double-needle stitching on the shoulders and hem, so the seams survive the daily grind.

Prints That Land

High-density screen prints and custom woven brand tags — details that read clearly and keep their colour.

Street-Fit Sizing

Relaxed, oversized and drop-shoulder cuts across S, M, L, XL and XXL so the fit always lands the same way.
